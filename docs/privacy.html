FocusLock Privacy Policy

Last updated: REPLACE_WITH_DATE

FocusLock ("the app") helps you stay focused by turning your phone into a calls-only screen during sessions. This policy explains what the app does with information. Contact: REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_EMAIL

Information that stays on your device

  • Your focus sessions (dates and minutes), streaks and awards.
  • Your settings: accent color, optional background image, reminder choice.
  • A background image you choose is copied into the app's private storage. It is never uploaded.

We do not have an account system and we do not receive this information. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions and what they are used for

  • Home app role: lets FocusLock be your Home screen so only Phone, Contacts and Emergency are shown during a session. You can change your Home app in Settings at any time. The app does not read what is on your screen.
  • Do Not Disturb access: silences notifications during a session and restores your previous setting afterwards. The app does not read your notifications.
  • Notifications (optional): used for the weekly focus report and the music player control.
  • Internet: used to show ads in the free version, to check the purchase, and to download a small settings file (it contains no personal data).

Ads (free version)

The free version may show ads on the Stats, Awards and Style screens, never during a focus session. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and ad-interaction data to show ads and measure them. In the EEA, UK and some other regions you are asked for consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See Google's privacy policy. Subscribing to Pro removes ads.

Purchases

Payments and subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. We receive confirmation that you have an active subscription so we can unlock Pro. You can cancel any time in Google Play, under Payments & subscriptions.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. When you tap Share, the app creates an image and hands it to the app you choose.

Children

FocusLock is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the app listing.
